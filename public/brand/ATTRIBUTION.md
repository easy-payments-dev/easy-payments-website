## Provenance

Official Easy Payments brand files were copied from the workspace folder
`C:\Users\jantv\Desktop\projects\easy-payments-dev`:

| Workspace file | Preserved source | Web derivative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Easy Payments Logo.png` | `brand-assets/source/easy-payments-logo.png` | `logo-lockup-640.png`, `og-image.png` |
| `Navy App Icon with Teal Bars and White P.png` | `brand-assets/source/easy-payments-mark.png` | `mark-64.png`, `mark-128.png`, favicon set |
| `easy-payments-icon-under-1mb.png` | `brand-assets/source/easy-payments-mark-alt.png` | alternate mark archive |

`favicon.svg` is a lightweight SVG adaptation of the official navy / turquoise / white EP mark for sharp small sizes.

Header/footer lockups (`header-lockup-*.png`, `logo-lockup-*.png`) were refreshed from
`Navy App Icon with Teal Bars and White P.png` so the mark’s **P stays white** against the navy
rounded square (the original lockup export had a near-invisible dark P). Teal E bars and the
wordmark were preserved.
